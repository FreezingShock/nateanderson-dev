## Standing notes
- **Cannot be tested in the cloud (his devices):** Safari/WebKit, real phones (touch, safe-area, performance), audio, the one-tab rule across two real tabs, and the live site (the password gate returns 401 to threads).
- **Left alone on purpose:** do not add x10 or max buy buttons, tap clickers, third-party art or sound, a coloured strip on a panel's left edge, or pill chips and tabs. Scramble text never sticks.
- **Performance:** the audit reports are in `/mnt/project-files/portfolio/perf-audit-2026-11-15/starwright.md` and `/mnt/project-files/portfolio/starwright/`. Play runs at 60 fps with no long frames at 4x CPU; what was left was load on a slow phone and idle main-thread animation cost. PERF-ui-preload was tried and dropped; it is to be revisited only after PERF-ssr-skeleton, which is done, and needs a re-measure first.
