# Node Farm

An original idle incremental game at `/creations/games/node-farm`, in the Games hub's Incremental section. Theme: you hack
one node from a naOS terminal and grow it into a farm. Saves in the browser (`localStorage` key `nf-save-v1`).

## The chain
Bytes (per second) <- Multipliers <- Reboots <- Copper <- Silicon <- Quartz <- Aether <- Runes <- Starglass <- Voidsteel <- Chrono <- Nebula <- Pulsars <- Singularity <- Engrams <- Paradox <- Origin. Every stat except Bytes has six
button tiers (+1, +5, +25, +125, +625, +3125; each tier costs about 8 to 10 times the last, and a button's price grows 9 to 12 percent each time it is bought).
- Multipliers (paid in Bytes) add straight to Bytes per second.
- Reboots (paid in Multipliers) reset Bytes and Multipliers and make every Multiplier button hit harder.
- Copper, Silicon, Quartz and Aether are producers: their buttons add nodes that mine the stat every second. Copper is paid
  in Bytes, each later material in the one before it. Each material boosts the one below it (Copper boosts Reboot power and
  adds a little to Bytes, Silicon boosts Copper, Quartz boosts Silicon, Aether boosts Quartz).
- Runes, Starglass, Voidsteel and Chrono are the deep materials (unlock at 150 Aether, 200 Runes, 250 Starglass, 300 Voidsteel; a quarter of that after your first Ascend). Same rules as the others, each boosts the one below, Chrono adds a little to everything. They also add to the AP you earn (`TUNE.apPer`).
- Zones 12 to 17 (Nate, 2026-11-12: deep space first, then the Origin arc): Cloud Deck (Nebula), Beacon Spire (Pulsars), Event Horizon (Singularity), Engram Archive (Engrams), Mirror Stair (Paradox), Root Chamber (Origin). Same rules as the deep materials, each boosts the one below (`boostK` 0.12) and adds AP. Their extras grow with log10 of the amount (`TUNE.logK`) so they cannot run away: Singularity adds a little to everything, Engrams speed up Drones, Paradox strengthens Overclock, Origin multiplies AP on Ascend. Never give a late material a power-law "everything" boost: in the sim two of them blew every amount to Infinity within minutes. `tick` also caps every amount at `CAP` (1e300). Goal tier VIII "Source" holds the first of each. Art: icons and tier sprites in nodefarm-icons.py, zone scenes in scripts/naos-art/zone-art-12-17.py. Each has its own layered era scenery (see Scenery). Sim (with the lab): Nebula ~22-29 h, Origin ~43 h, tree ~44-46 h.
- Zones 18 to 25 (Nate, 2026-11-12: both arcs, four zones each, after the first Handover): **Overgrowth** Spore Cellar (Spores), Mycel Web (Mycelium), Canopy Hall (Pollen), Bloom Core (Bloom), then **Descent** Bedrock Shaft (Basalt), Magma Line (Magma), Geode Vault (Geodes), The Buried Sun (Sunstone, id `sunstone`; it was Core / `core` until Nate's rename pick, and `parseSave` moves old `core` saves over). `ARC_MATS` in engine.ts: `isUnlocked` keeps them shut until `hand.count > 0`, then they open like the deep chain (`unlockAt` on the amount below). Same rules: each boosts the one below (`boostK` 0.12) and adds a little AP. Extras are log10 like the Origin arc: Bloom multiplies Keys on Handover (`bloomKeys`), Sunstone adds a little to everything (`sunstoneAll`). Goal tier IX "Bedrock" holds the first of each. Art: page scenery `scripts/naos-art/nodefarm-scenes-arc.py` (eras 15 to 22, layered like 9 to 14), 64 px button sprites and zone icons `scripts/naos-art/nodefarm_arc64.py` (stubs `nodefarm_tiers64_<stat>.py`), zone panels `scripts/naos-art/zone-art-18-25.py` (built from the era scene and the button sprites). Arcs registered in `lib/nodefarm/arcs.ts`.
- Prestige tree (see "Prestige tree" below): 40 nodes in five sections, bought with AP, kept through Ascend. `state.tree` holds owned ids; old saves are refunded for nodes that no longer exist.
- Ascend (400 Aether): wipes everything except goals, perks and lifetime stats for Ascension Points (+30 percent to everything each) and five perks.
- There is no tapping: progress comes from buttons only (Nate did not want a Cookie Clicker). The core is a live display: its outer arc shows progress to the cheapest Reboot and it pulses on every press. Quick-time events (see below) appear every 3 to 5 minutes; clearing one gives the timed Overclock plus a rarity loot roll.
- Offline progress is capped at 8 hours (Standby cells perk: +4 h per level). Nothing is auto-bought. Returning after more than a minute away opens a full-screen standby report (components/nodefarm/away.tsx): time banked, a standby-window meter, what each resource gained, and a Collect button. Everything is already banked when it opens; Esc or Collect closes it. `lib/nodefarm/offline.ts` (`catchUp`) wraps the engine's `offline` and reads what each system did, shown as plain data groups under the resource tiles (Scripts, Drone hangar, Zone rigs, Exploit Lab, Zone events). What runs while away: production, Drones (away speed), Scripts except ascend.sh, rig meters, Lab Intel and the compile queue, and the clocks of events and payloads. What does not: quick-time events, new zone events (a running one only runs out) and anything that needs a choice. Steps are 10 s while Scripts are owned (20 s otherwise), and a step is cut at the moment a timed boost ends (Overclock, Swarm alarm, zone event, payload), so a boost counts only for the time it had left. Check: /mnt/project-files/portfolio/node-farm-offline/ (report.mjs, offline-check.mjs).

## Gotchas
- Always put `font` rules for buttons through `:where(button)` (the reset is), or element selectors beat the class rules.
- Events only spawn once Multipliers plus Reboots reach 5 (the trace needs 8), so a brand-new player is not interrupted. The incoming card waits 15 s, so a Playwright test that fast-forwards a fake clock must step in small chunks and stop as soon as `.nf-ev` exists.

## Goals, secrets, Records and the console (`nf`)
- Goals: seven tiers of six (Access to Zero). Each done goal or found secret gives +2% to everything, a full tier +8%. `state.achAt` holds when each was done (the log shows it). Tiers 6 and 7 cover the deep materials, the tree and long grinds.
- Secrets (`SECRETS` in engine.ts) show as ???? with a clue. Three are unlocked by hidden console commands (`nf ping 127.0.0.1`, `nf sudo`, `nf whoami`), three by play (3 am session, 24 h played, a full standby report).
- Records window (dock and a phone tab): this run, materials, lifetime, progress. Built in `lib/nodefarm/records.ts` (`recordSections`).
- Console: `nf`, `nf stats`, `nf goals`, `nf secrets`, `nf open`. The console asks the open game for its state with an `nf:live` event (the game fills `detail.live`); with no game open it reads and writes the saved game in localStorage. `nfCommand` is pure, so new commands go in records.ts.
- New life counters (`capped`, `peakBps`, `apTotal`) and `achAt` are filled in for old saves by `parseSave`.

## Drones (automation)
One Drone per button tier (T1 to T6), bought with Aether, up to level 10 (`TUNE.drone`). Each level presses that tier's button 0.25 times a second on every stat the player has armed (Armed switch in each stat zone; Reboots are never automated). Drones reset on Ascend, the armed list is kept. Time away runs them at 50% speed and the standby report shows the Drone presses. The Autoloader perk now covers Multipliers and Copper through Chrono (max 9 levels).

## Drone hangar (postings, mastery, mods, drone tree)
Drones grow on three clocks (engine section "Drone hangar", every number in `DRONE_TUNE`; state `s.dr`, `DroneState`):
- Postings and zone mastery (kept forever). Each tier's Drone either sweeps (presses its tier on every armed zone, as before) or is posted to one zone (`setPost`, `postOf`): it presses only there, `postX` (3) times as fast, and every press it attempts there adds service to that zone (`s.dr.svc`). Service sets the zone's mastery rank I to V (`rankAt`), and each rank multiplies the zone's output (`rankX`, applied in `derive`: `rates[m]` and `multGainMult`). Postings stay through Ascend; a posting to a zone that is locked again sweeps until it reopens. Covers all 15 button zones, Multipliers to Origin.
- Parts and mods (one run). Every press a Drone attempts salvages `partK` Parts. Parts buy five mods per Drone (`MODS`, `buyMod`, `MOD_COUNT`): Servo (speed), Uplink (posting speed), Reach (also presses the tier below), Tuner (mastery builds `tunerEach` faster while posted) and Salvager (`salvageEach` more Parts). Old saves with three mod slots are padded in `parseDr`. Parts and mods reset on Ascend (`drOnAscend`), Black box keeps half.
- Mastery reaches into other systems: each zone at rank V adds `masteredSpeed` to every Drone (`masteredZones`, in `hangarSpeed`); each rank cuts that zone's rig upgrade price by `rigCut` (`rigMasteryCut`, used in `rigPrice` in rigs.ts); while a Drone is posted to a zone, that zone's good events last `eventLonger` longer and faults clear `faultFaster` faster (`droneEventPace`, used in `zeStep` in zoneevents.ts). New zones join mastery on their own through `POSTABLE`.
- Drone tree (kept forever). 18 nodes in three arms (Flight, Dispatch, Salvage) on the shared `TreeMenu`, theme `hangar` (amber skin and hangar-floor backdrop: `nodefarm-hangar.css`, `hangar-backdrop.tsx`). Cores = mastery ranks earned minus nodes owned (`cores`), so nothing can drift; respec is free (`respecDrones`). Effects in `droneFx` (cached on the owned list).
- UI: `components/nodefarm/drones.tsx` (berth strip with live sprites: parked, sweeping drift, posted sorties; wallet; roster with Posting select and mod buttons; zone mastery lines; `droneModel`). Posted Drones and mastery also show on each zone's Drones line (`PostedHere` in stat-row.tsx). Feed lines `drones.post / sweep / refit`, `hangar.fit / mastery`, a banner on each rank up. Guide step `post-drone` once after the first Drone. Admin `nf admin max` maxes the hangar too; `clearHangar` in admin.ts wipes it.
- Art: Drone sprites are 64px, 8 frames per tier in `scripts/naos-art/nodefarm-drones64.py` (`drones.png`, 384x512; `.nf-sp-d` steps through the frames); `scripts/naos-art/nodefarm-hangar-art.py` (hangar-bay, hangar-bg-0, hangar-pad), tree sprites `dx_*` in `scripts/naos-art/nodefarm_drone_motifs.py` (appended last in the sheets).
- Pacing (sim with the hangar bot, `NF_NOHANGAR=1` skips it): first Ascend 4.0 h to 2.8 h, Chrono 13.7 h to 14 h, Origin and whole tree 46 h to about 39 h. The bot re-posts every 2 minutes to the lowest-mastery zone, so a real player gains less. Biggest knob is `rankX` (mastery compounds through the chain boosts: a first try at x2.2 for rank V took the whole game to 25 h).

## Code reveal (lib/nodefarm/cx.ts, app/camino/nodefarm-cx.css)
One open/close animation for every window, card and pop-up ("terminal print"): the frame opens out of a thin line, corner brackets snap in, a scan line sweeps, rows marked `data-cx-i` print left to right with a caret, top to bottom, and `data-cx-scr` labels decode out of glyphs (the engine's `scrambleNow`, always lands on the real text). Closing deletes rows bottom-up, then folds the frame back to a line. About 280 ms in, 160 ms out, 40 ms per row, at most 8 staggered rows, only rows on screen animate.
- `codeReveal(el, { dir, rows, ms, bare })` returns a Promise; a new call on the same element restarts (never queues); a deadline timer always settles it; reduced motion resolves at once. `cxPlay(el)` replays the open, `cxStop(el)` lands it now, `cxClear(el)` lifts what an "out" left (hidden).
- `useCodeReveal(ref, open)` keeps a thing mounted through its exit.
- `--cx-c` sets the bracket/caret/scan colour (falls back to `--c`, then cyan). `bare` skips the frame for things that sit inline.
- Windows (Map, Goals, Records, Ascend, Menu) go through `closeWin()` in node-farm.tsx; on phones a tab switch prints the section's rows in. Put `data-cx-i` on a panel's sections to control the print order; without any, the pane's second-level children print.
- `data-cx-scr` replaces the text node, so only put it on static labels (use an sr-only twin like WinBar does).
- Where it is used: windows (node-farm.tsx), the offline report (away.tsx, rows tagged, backdrop fades), quick-time event cards (events.tsx: prints in, reprints the body on each stage, deletes out while the last state stays on screen inert), banners (juice.tsx, timing unchanged), tooltips (guide.tsx `Tip`: reprints when the target changes, deletes out on leave), folding zones and the Drone bay (`useFoldReveal`, rows only, first render skipped). The tree card and the Ascend window use it from their own files.
- Sound: `nfPrint` (soft key ticks, one per row) on a framed "in", `nfDelete` on a framed "out"; bare reveals are silent. Pass `sound` to override.
- Three styles, each with its job (Nate, 2026-11-12): `print` for milestones (banners), tooltips and the tree card; `decrypt` (rows under flickering glyph noise that resolves left to right) for unlock and tier banners and quick-time event cards; `crt` (monitor switch on/off; folds flicker) for panels: windows, phone tab sections, the offline report and folding zones. Callers pass `style` (default print). `nf anim <style>` or `?cx=<style>` forces one style everywhere for testing; `nf anim auto` clears it. CRT animates `transform`, so it fits elements that position with `translate`/`left`, not `transform`.
- Banners sit under an open window (z-index 55 while `body.nf-win-open`) instead of covering it.

## Game feel (components/nodefarm/juice.tsx, app/camino/nodefarm-juice.css)
- The game talks to it with `juice.emit({ t: "press" | "tier" | "unlock" | "ascend" })` and `checkMilestones(s, bps)` (called twice a second from the loop). It never touches the engine or the save.
- Press pop-ups: plain DOM nodes in a fixed layer on `<body>` (no React state), tinted by stat, a few dither sparks, bigger from tier 4 up and on Reboots. Max 14 alive; held-button repeats merge into one pop every 300 ms. They appear over the last pressed `.nf-btn`.
- Banners (one at a time, queued, unlocks jump the queue): first-ever stat decrypt (full, with the stat's role), repeat decrypts and first purchase of a tier (short), and milestones (Bytes/s powers, presses, Reboots, Ascensions). What was already seen is kept per device in `nf-juice-v1`; the first call on a device only records, so returning players get no pile-up. Add milestones to `MS`.
- Ascend: a full-screen sequence on `<body>` that charges a scaled-up reactor core (magenta flicker), flashes once, then types "Root access granted" and counts up the AP. Click, Enter, Esc or Space skips the charge, then closes; it closes itself after 14 s.
- Reduced motion: no pop-ups, scramble, flash or count-up; banners and the Ascend card just appear. Haptics (Android) only after the first tap.
- Class names use the `nf-pop`, `nf-jb` and `nf-rx` prefixes (`nf-asc*` already belongs to the Ascend panel).

## Guidance (next step, locked zones, tooltips, progress map)
- `lib/nodefarm/guide.ts` (pure, reads only the engine): `nextStep` picks the one thing to do now (four TRAINING steps, then the chain zone by zone, Drones, Ascend, spending AP, then the nearest goal), `unlockInfo` says what opens a locked zone and how far along you are, `buttonTip` / `droneTip` build tooltip content from the engine's own `button` and `derive`, so a rebalance in `TUNE` needs no change here. Reboot unlock numbers are `REBOOT_UNLOCK` in the engine.
- `components/nodefarm/guide.tsx`: the always-visible strip under the HUD (title, why, progress bar with ETA, "Show me" opens the zone and scrolls to the glowing button, "Map") and the tooltip layer (`TipCtx`, one `.nf-tip` at the root of `.nf`, positioned from the button's box because quartz buttons are clip-pathed). Hover and keyboard focus on desktop; on touch the tip flashes for 2.6 s after a tap, so an unaffordable button explains itself.
- Glow: `data-guide` on the targeted button, zone, dock button or tab. Only the first locked zone is drawn in full (with a progress meter); the rest fold into one "BEYOND" line, and the Drone bay appears once Aether is open.
- `components/nodefarm/map.tsx`: the Map window (dock button, a tab on phones, MAP in the strip): signal chain Bytes to Chrono with state and boosts, Ascend / Drones / Goals cards, and a summary of the prestige tree sections and a button into the tree menu. Saves are unchanged (nf-save-v1).

## Prestige tree (full-screen menu)
- **40 nodes, 8 per section** (Nate, 2026-11-12: fewer, bigger, unique nodes), plus 20 deeper nodes (below). Data in `ARMS` in `lib/nodefarm/engine.ts`: each row is slot, id, kind, name, effect (`blurb`), flavour line (`flavor`), effect data (`fx`). Every node does one distinct thing; six switch on whole systems (`fx.feature`, checked with `hasFeature`). The node list Nate can edit lives in his doc "Node Farm prestige tree: new node list".
- **Deeper tree (2026-11-12, 20 more nodes, 60 in all).** Three node kinds on top of the first 40, set by `form` on the node:
  - Junctions (5, `JUNCTIONS`): halfway between two sections at 7.4 units, linked to arm slot 6 and the next arm's slot 5. `req` lists both, and `nodeOpen` needs all of them. A junction shows (locked, card "needs both") once you own either side. Effects are cross-section synergies: Bus bar (materials grow with log10 of Reboots), Manifest (+5% AP per material produced), Night crew (+1 Drone level cap per goal tier done), Swarm alarm (event win = Drones x4 for 60 s, `state.frenzy`), Feedback loop (Reboot power x3 during Overclock).
  - Forks (10, slots 8 and 9 in `OUTER`): two past each keystone, `excl` points at the other side. Owning one locks the other; buying the other is a free swap (`buyNode` refunds the first side). Pairs: Cold start / Long haul (Bytes after a Reboot, `state.rebootAt`, `runBytes`), Specialist / Generalist, Hoarder / Investor, Wide net / Spearhead, Storm chaser / Patient.
  - Ranked (5, slot 10): one tip per section, opens from either fork, bought up to `ranks` (5) times. Ranks above 1 live in `state.treeLv` (kept through Ascend, cleared by respec); `nodeLevel`, `nodeCost` (price x`TUNE.rankCost` per rank), `nodeMaxed`. `treeFx` applies a ranked node's fx once per rank; its cache key includes the ranks.
  - "Whole tree" means `TREE_FULL` (55: one side of each fork; ranks not needed), `treeComplete(s)`, `fullTreeIds()`. Story section logs still count only the first 8 nodes of a section (`!n.form`). Old saves keep everything; `migrateTree` also drops a doubled fork side and bad rank values.
  - Pacing (sim with the Exploit Lab, `NF_ASC_GAP=1` so the bot Ascends at most hourly): first 40 at about 51 h, every node at about 54 h, every rank maxed at about 60 h (Nate picked "about +10 h", 2026-11-12). Costs: junctions 160, forks 400, ranks 200 x1.6 per rank. The late game still snowballs once the first 40 are done (AP per Ascend jumps from about 150 to 5,000 within a few hours), so the ranks are priced for that.
  - Menu: a mono tag above junctions and forks ("junction", "fork", "swap"), five rank pips above ranked nodes, card lines for needs both / fork / ranked, the buy button reads Swap or Rank N. Test: /mnt/project-files/portfolio/node-farm-tree/deep/deep.mjs.
- Layout: each section is a diamond along its arm (`SLOTS`): entry at 2.4 units, an upper path (slots 1, 3, 5) and a lower path (2, 4, 6), keystone at 9.6 units. No bridges between sections. Spacing is at least 1.87 units between any two nodes. Node fields the menu and art read: `kind` (minor, notable, keystone), `flavor`, `sprite` (= id), `ix` (index in `TREE`), `entry`, `chain` (Carrier I to III, Mounted drive I and II).
- Reveal: `nodeVis(s, n)` gives `shown` (owned or buyable: touches the core or an owned node), `fog` (one step past a shown node: a silhouette, no name or cost) or `hidden`.
- Costs: `SLOT_COST` by slot (4 to 140 AP), notables x1.3, the Rubber duck 1 AP, all scaled by `TUNE.treeCost`. **Respec** (`respec`) is free, refunds every AP and clears the Mounted drives.
- AP bonus: +30% per AP held, so spending AP lowers it until respec (Nate kept this 2026-11-12; `TUNE.apBonusFromEarned` would count spent AP too, but the sim then finishes the tree in 12 to 19 h). Carrier nodes raise the per-AP value (`apEach`). `TUNE.apGainSoft` puts a log knee on Ascends above 250 AP so AP cannot run off to infinity after the tree is done.
- Pacing (sim): first Ascend 3.3 h, whole tree 60.3 h without Drones, 75 h with the bot arming Drones (Nate's target: about 60 h).
- Effects (`treeFx`, cached per tree array): boosts written "xN" multiply with each other (`bytes`, `global`, `reboot`, `mats`, `ap`, `drone`, `ocTime`, `ocX`). The others: `growth` (prices climb slower), `rebootKeep`, `unlockMul`, starting kits (`kitReboot`, `kitProd`, `kitDrones`, applied in `ascend`), `droneKeep`, `droneGlobal`, `mounts`, `eventRate` (`eventHaste`), `winBoost` (`state.runWins`), `zeroDay` and `echo` (returned by `breach`). `treeTotals(s)` turns them into readout rows for the menu.
- Systems: Watchdog timer (`watchdogStep` in `tick`, switch in the Reboot zone, `state.watchdog`), Forecast (time to afford on each button), Dead drop (`dropFill` / `openDrop`, `state.dropAt`, HUD meter; real time, fills while away), Standing orders (`autoArm`, `state.autoArmed` so a stat you disarm stays off), Mounted drives (`setMount`, `state.mounts`, picker on the Ascend pane), Ghost line, Cross-talk, Echo (the event layer brings the next event right after the result), Zero-day (`payout`).
- Old saves: `parseSave` calls `migrateTree`, which drops ids that are not in the tree and refunds them at their old price (`LEGACY_COST`, the 60 nodes from #157). ## Quick-time events
`lib/nodefarm/events.ts` holds the event list, every timing number (`EVENT_TUNE`) and the loot maths; `components/nodefarm/events.tsx` is the `EventLayer` that schedules them; styles are in `app/camino/nodefarm-events.css`. The layer is portaled to `<body>` (or a full-screen element smaller than the page), so the card centers on the visible screen whatever the scroll. Flow: incoming card (Engage / Skip, 15 s then it passes, controls lock for 0.65 s so a stray tap can't start one) then the live event then a loot result card.

Nate dislikes reaction, memory, puzzle and guessing games, so no event tests reflexes (the four from #145, Intrusion, Power surge, Signal lock and Breach trace, were retired for that). Five types, each with its own colour and dithered canvas art:
- Traffic spike (cyan): the card docks as a strip above the site dock and you play the real board; every buy press fills a 15-press contract. Cash out any time.
- Rogue process (purple): pick one of two or three shown outcomes (Quarantine = Bytes, Exploit = extra Overclock, Harvest = newest material), all at one rolled rarity.
- Cold storage (green): hold to decrypt six rows of a hex dump; let go to pause, cash out any time, every open row counts.
- Relay cascade (amber): five relays charge in turn; a full relay waits for you; pressing within 0.9 s of it filling builds the chain.
- Dead packet (pink): three presses crack it open.

Each body reports a quality from 0 to 1; when the clock runs out it reports what it has so far. From `winAt` (0.25) it is a win: node-farm.tsx calls `breach()` (Overclock, so the Anomaly tree nodes keep working) then `payLoot()`. Loot rarity uses the board's six rarity colours; better quality shifts the roll up. Loot is a lump of Bytes or of the newest material (seconds of plain, non-Overclock output) or extra Overclock seconds, sized so engaging every event adds about +40% over the Overclock alone. Skipping or closing with too little done costs nothing. Touch, mouse and keyboard all work (1 to 3, Space, Escape skips); the card is an `alertdialog` (the docked spike strip is a `status`) and returns focus when it closes. Events can be switched off from the card or the Menu (`nf-events-v1`). Admin `nf:fire` shows any kind right away. To add a type: add it to `EVENTS`, add a body that calls `useDone(props, sofar)`, and style it in nodefarm-events.css. ## Board render holds (2026-11-12, perf)
The game state is one mutable object and `NodeFarm` re-renders 10 times a second, so everything under it re-rendered too. `components/nodefarm/hold.tsx` (`Hold live k`) keeps returning a part's last element while it is not `live` and its key `k` is unchanged, so React skips it. Used for: side windows (map, goals, records, ascend, handover, stress, codex, menu, and their title bars) while hidden, the body of a folded zone (`StatRow`), Dock, phone tabs, Chain and arc heads (about 3 Hz), and the Lab, Scripts and Hangar panels (3 Hz folded, 5 Hz open). Anything a player does bumps `ver`, which refreshes held parts at once. The 10 Hz loop uses `tickBump`, not `bump`, for the same reason: add new player actions with `bump`. `BuyBtn` computes what it prints and `BuyView` is memoised on those strings. `useOffscreenPause` (offscreen.ts) sets `data-off` on panels scrolled out of view and `nodefarm-fx.css` pauses their loops. When adding a panel that nobody sees most of the time, wrap it in `Hold`; do not read `s` in a held element's event handlers through stale closures (read `g.current`). Numbers and harness: portfolio/node-farm-perf/board/.
## Sound pass (mixer)

Every Node Farm sound goes through `nfx(name, arg?, system?)` in `lib/nodefarm/sound.ts`, never `sfx` directly. It belongs to one of eight systems (buttons, unlocks, runs, automation, events, world, interface, hum) and plays at that system's volume, kept per device in `nf-mix-v1` (not in the save). The site-wide master volume and sound switch still apply first. Sliders are in the Menu window's settings under "mix"; clicking a name plays a sample. Under the hood `setLevel()` in `lib/sound/engine.ts` scales the gain of every voice created during that one `play()`.

To add a sound: write it in `lib/sound/sounds.ts` (same voices: key clicks, square blips, soft sine bodies, filtered sweeps), give it a home system in `HOME` in `lib/nodefarm/sound.ts`, and call `nfx("name")`. The new voices are `nfHandover`, `nfStress`, `nfScript` (arg `{ on }`), `nfDrone`, `nfRig`, `nfZone` (arg `{ good }`), `nfCache`, `nfVent`, `nfPage` and `nfEnd` (reserved for the ending screen: call `nfx("nfEnd")`). Milestone banners also play the goal chirp, and the rate limiter drops it when something louder just played.

## Engine tests (tests/nodefarm, 2026-11-12)

`npm run test:nodefarm` runs the engine tests with Node's built-in runner (`node:test`, no new dependencies; Node 22 strips the TypeScript itself). It takes about 25 s and runs on every PR as a step of the blocking "Typecheck, build, link crawl" job, before the build, so a red run blocks the merge like a type error does. Run it before you push any change under `lib/nodefarm/`. One file alone: `node --experimental-strip-types --no-warnings --test tests/nodefarm/resets.test.mjs`.

| File | What it pins down |
|---|---|
| `saves.test.mjs` | Every save in `scripts/nodefarm/fixtures` loads (plain JSON and base64) with nothing lost (runs `save-compat.mjs`, `removed-nodes-check.mjs` and `backups-check.mjs`), export then import gives the same save, bad input never throws |
| `resets.test.mjs` | What Reboot, Ascend, Handover and a stress test keep and wipe; a refused Ascend or Handover changes nothing; a stress test puts the real run back exactly |
| `run.test.mjs` | A bot playing 4 h with Ascends, a maxed state for 1 h and two Handover cycles never makes NaN, Infinity or a negative balance; away time (10 min, 1 h, 8 h, 24 h) stays inside the cap and never shrinks lifetime Bytes; lifetime Bytes, presses and Reboots agree between 0.1 s and 4 s ticks (10% for Bytes, 1% for presses) |
| `zones.test.mjs` | 25 zones in order, unlock chain never skips, zones 18 to 25 stay shut until the first Handover |
| `systems.test.mjs` | Scripts, drones, rigs, quick-time events, zone events (every kind in every zone), the lab, goals, secrets, the prestige tree and fork swaps do not throw or make NaN; ids are unique and every tree, lab, Firmware and drone node can be reached from the core |

Rules for tests: they must not change game behaviour, and the keep/wipe rules in `resets.test.mjs` are the source of truth for what a reset keeps, so change them in the same PR as the engine change. `tests/nodefarm/helpers.mjs` has the loader (it hooks module resolution like the bundler does), `maxed()` (a late-game state built with admin's `maxOut`), `play()` (a greedy bot) and `problems()` (NaN and negatives). To add a save fixture, run `scripts/nodefarm/save-gen.mjs` against an older build and drop the JSON in `scripts/nodefarm/fixtures`; every test that loops over fixtures picks it up. Known cost: a maxed state with all Drones is slow to tick (about 60 ms a tick), so tests keep those runs short.

## Reset rules (keep and wipe)

What each reset keeps and wipes is checked by `tests/nodefarm/resets.test.mjs` (runs in `npm run test:nodefarm`) and printed field by field by `scripts/nodefarm/reset-audit.mjs`. The confirm screens and the Handover ledger say the same thing; `scripts/nodefarm/reset-browser.mjs` checks those screens on a production build.

- **Reboot** wipes Bytes, Multipliers and Multiplier tiers, banks the crash-dump Intel, and consumes an armed Logic bomb. Everything else stays.
- **Ascend** wipes the run, Drones, Parts, Intel, Reboots (to the kit) and the production. It keeps goals, achievements, logs, perks, the tree, lab research and stock, scripts, the hangar's postings, Keys, Firmware, rig levels, the stress test ranks, arc progress and lifetime stats.
- **Handover** wipes everything Ascend wipes, plus AP, perks (half with Perk cache), the tree (Imprint and Deep imprint keep some nodes), Mounted drives, Lab Intel and stock, rig levels (half with Rig memory), and the hangar's Parts and mod levels (part with Flight recorder and Black box). It keeps what Ascend keeps, minus the lab image, and zone event history.
- **Stress test start** puts the real run aside and starts a fresh one. **Leave** puts it back. Goals, achievements, logs, scripts, lifetime stats, cleared ranks and which tiers were pressed come along. Everything else from inside the test is discarded.
- **Locked during a test:** Ascend, Handover, the prestige tree, perks and Firmware (buys and respec).
- **Tree respec** refunds AP and clears Mounted drives. **Firmware respec** refunds Keys. **Drone tree respec** refunds Cores.
- **Wipe save** replaces the save with a fresh one. **Import** and **restore** replace it, after a backup. Backups: Ascend, Handover, wipe, import, restore and stress test starts are `event` backups (newest 3).
